# Huawei MatePad 11 / Android 12L — research milestone 2026.10.29

By **Queen_Glasser (@glasses666)**.

This release packages the Android 10 → 11 → 12L investigation for the Huawei MatePad 11
(2021, DBY-W09, Snapdragon 865).

## Accepted on the research device

- Android 12L boot completion and repeated cold boots with SELinux Enforcing,
  vendor verity and existing root retained.
- Display/touch/GPU, Wi-Fi, camera, sensor events, Bluetooth discovery and microphone input.
- Four-byte speaker-detector repair, audible mono/stereo and boot-persistent compatibility.
- Systemless Google services and real Google account addition.
- Browser → GMS → USB YubiKey → CTAP2 assertion → successful ChatGPT login after
  restoring one GMS runtime permission.

## Downloads

The kit contains reviewed source, tests, offline compatibility builders, provenance/citation
metadata, recovery notes and a per-file SHA-256 inventory. The speaker module builder consumes
the owner's exact locally extracted library. ZIP/tar archives are research downloads, not
recovery-flashable ROMs. Consult [build and recovery](https://github.com/glasses666/dby-w09-boot-research/blob/v2026.10.29/docs/BUILD_AND_RECOVERY.md) for input
requirements and reconstruction limits.

Complete tested images contain third-party vendor components; the rooted boot also carries
installation-specific root material. Those images are not redistributed.

## Open checks and credit

Satellite positioning, USB-free suspend/resume, torch, Bluetooth pairing, long-duration
stability, certification and Android 13+ remain unaccepted. Termux passed a UID-level harness;
terminal-visible acceptance remains separate.
